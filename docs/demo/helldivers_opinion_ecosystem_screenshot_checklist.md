# Helldivers Opinion Ecosystem Screenshot Checklist

## Screenshot rules

Use screenshots to show the demo path clearly while keeping safety boundaries visible.

Required language to preserve in captions or nearby notes:

- frontend-only local demo
- selected public sample
- not full-web coverage
- not full-platform coverage
- not full-thread coverage
- not official verification
- not causal proof
- no real platform action
- no real API or LLM call
- PeopleCluster balls represent anonymous groups/clusters, not real individuals
- InfluenceCore nodes represent content / narrative / official / media / meme cores, not people balls

## Final screenshot pack checklist

- Filenames follow numeric order.
- At least one screenshot shows the data source selector.
- At least one screenshot shows sample limitations.
- At least one screenshot shows PeopleCluster and InfluenceCore together.
- At least one screenshot shows Mapping contract.
- At least three scenario screenshots are included.
- Captions do not claim full-web analysis.
- Captions do not claim production simulation.
- Captions do not claim official verification.
- Captions do not claim causal proof.
